🚨 The Top 10 Exam MCQ Traps:

  • 1. Drawings & Dividends: Direct reduction of Equity; NEVER an expense on the Income Statement!
  • 2. Sunk Costs: Past spent money (R&D, market surveys) is ALWAYS 100% irrelevant to decisions.
  • 3. Opportunity Costs: Foregone alternative income MUST ALWAYS be included in project appraisals.
  • 4. NPV vs IRR Conflict: When mutually exclusive, ALWAYS choose highest NPV (IRR ignores scale).
  • 5. Quick Ratio < 1.0x: Normal in paint or FMCG due to necessary heavy inventory holding (Asian Paints = 0.89x).
  • 6. Fixed Cost per Unit: Fixed cost in total is fixed; fixed cost per unit FALLS as volume rises.
  • 7. Price Cut Shock: A 10% price cut spikes break-even volume by +33.3% because variable costs are sticky!
  • 8. Inventory Turnover Denominator: Always use COGS, NOT Sales revenue!
  • 9. Forwards vs Futures: Forwards = OTC + high default risk; Futures = Exchange + Clearinghouse zero default.
  • 10. Depreciation: Non-cash expense; added back to Net Income to compute Operating Cash Flow.
