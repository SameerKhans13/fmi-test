📑 Official Exam Formula Reference Sheet

These formulas are permitted/provided during the exam. (Note: CVP formulas are excluded and must be memorized).

1. Liquidity & Working Capital Ratios

• Current Ratio: $\frac{\text{Current Assets}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$ (Benchmark: 1.5–2.0)

• Quick / Acid-Test Ratio: $\frac{\text{Current Assets} - \text{Inventories}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$

• Cash Ratio: $\frac{\text{Cash \& Cash Equivalents}}{\text{Current Liabilities}}$

• Operating Cycle: $\text{Inventory Days} + \text{Receivable Days} - \text{Payable Days}$

• Inventory Days: $\frac{365 \times \text{Average Inventory}}{\text{COGS}}$

• Receivable (Debtor) Days: $\frac{365 \times \text{Average Debtors}}{\text{Net Sales}}$

• Payable (Creditor) Days: $\frac{365 \times \text{Average Creditors}}{\text{COGS or Purchases}}$

2. Solvency & Coverage Ratios

• Debt-to-Equity (D/E): $\frac{\text{Total Interest-bearing Debt}}{\text{Total Shareholders' Equity}}$

• Interest Coverage Ratio: $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Interest Expense}}$ (Warning sign if $< 2.0\times$)

3. Profitability & DuPont Analysis

• Gross Margin: $\frac{\text{Gross Profit}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100$

• Operating Margin: $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100$

• Net Profit Margin: $\frac{\text{PAT}}{\text{Sales}} \times 100$

• ROCE: $\frac{\text{EBIT}}{\text{Capital Employed (Equity + Debt)}}$

• ROE (DuPont 3-Way): $\text{Net Profit Margin} \times \text{Asset Turnover} \times \text{Equity Multiplier}$

where $\text{Asset Turnover} = \frac{\text{Sales}}{\text{Total Assets}}$ and $\text{Equity Multiplier} = \frac{\text{Total Assets}}{\text{Equity}}$

4. Time Value of Money & Capital Budgeting

• Future Value (FV): $PV \times (1 + r)^n$

• Present Value (PV): $\frac{FV}{(1 + r)^n}$

• Ordinary Annuity PV: $PMT \times \left[\frac{1 - (1+r)^{-n}}{r}\right]$

• Level Perpetuity PV: $\frac{PMT}{r}$

• Growing Perpetuity PV: $\frac{CF_1}{r - g}$ (valid when $r > g$)

• Net Present Value (NPV): $\sum_{t=1}^n \frac{CF_t}{(1+r)^t} - \text{Initial Outlay}$

• Profitability Index (PI): $\frac{\text{PV of Future Cash Inflows}}{\text{Initial Outlay}}$

• Rule of 72: $\text{Years to Double} \approx \frac{72}{r\%}$

5. MUST-MEMORIZE Formulas (Excluded from sheet)

• Contribution: $\text{Selling Price} - \text{Variable Cost}$

• P/V Ratio: $\frac{\text{Contribution}}{\text{Sales}}$

• Break-Even Point (Units): $\frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{Unit Contribution}}$

• Break-Even Point (Value): $\frac{\text{Fixed Cost}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$

• Margin of Safety: $\text{Actual Sales} - \text{Break-Even Sales} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{\text{P/V Ratio}}$
