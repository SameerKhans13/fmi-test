📑 Official Exam Formula Reference Sheet

These formulas are permitted/provided during the exam. (Note: CVP formulas are excluded and must be memorized).

1. Liquidity & Working Capital Ratios

• Current Ratio: Current Assets / Current Liabilities (Benchmark: 1.5–2.0×)

• Quick / Acid-Test Ratio: (Current Assets − Inventories) / Current Liabilities

• Cash Ratio: Cash & Cash Equivalents / Current Liabilities

• Operating Cycle: Inventory Days + Receivable Days − Payable Days

• Inventory Days: (365 × Average Inventory) / COGS

• Receivable (Debtor) Days: (365 × Average Debtors) / Net Sales

• Payable (Creditor) Days: (365 × Average Creditors) / COGS

2. Solvency & Coverage Ratios

• Debt-to-Equity (D/E): Total Interest-bearing Debt / Total Shareholders' Equity

• Interest Coverage Ratio: EBIT / Interest Expense (Warning sign if < 2.0×)

3. Profitability & DuPont Analysis

• Gross Margin: (Gross Profit / Sales) × 100

• Operating Margin: (EBIT / Sales) × 100

• Net Profit Margin: (PAT / Sales) × 100

• ROCE: EBIT / Capital Employed (Equity + Debt)

• ROE (DuPont 3-Way): Net Profit Margin × Asset Turnover × Equity Multiplier

where Asset Turnover = Sales / Total Assets and Equity Multiplier = Total Assets / Equity

4. Time Value of Money & Capital Budgeting

• Future Value (FV): PV × (1 + r)ⁿ

• Present Value (PV): FV / (1 + r)ⁿ

• Ordinary Annuity PV: PMT × [(1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ) / r]

• Level Perpetuity PV: PMT / r

• Growing Perpetuity PV: CF₁ / (r − g) (valid when r > g)

• Net Present Value (NPV): Σ [CFₜ / (1 + r)ᵗ] − Initial Outlay

• Profitability Index (PI): PV of Future Cash Inflows / Initial Outlay

• Rule of 72: Years to Double ≈ 72 / Interest Rate %

5. MUST-MEMORIZE Formulas (Excluded from sheet)

• Contribution: Selling Price − Variable Cost

• P/V Ratio: Contribution / Sales = (SP − VC) / SP

• Break-Even Point (Units): Fixed Cost / Unit Contribution

• Break-Even Point (Value): Fixed Cost / P/V Ratio

• Margin of Safety: Actual Sales − Break-Even Sales = Profit / P/V Ratio
